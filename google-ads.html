The Problem

Why Most Pain Clinic Google Ads Campaigns Fail

Generic Campaigns

Running one campaign for "pain management" misses the patient searching for "knee pain treatment options" or "chronic back pain specialist." Treatment-specific intent gets ignored.

Poor Conversion Tracking

If you can't track which keywords drive actual appointments (not just form fills from people asking about insurance), you're flying blind and wasting budget.

Ignoring the Patient Journey

Someone researching treatment options needs different messaging than someone ready to book today. Most campaigns treat everyone the same—and lose both.

Our Approach

Treatment-Specific Campaigns That Actually Convert

We structure Google Ads campaigns around how pain patients actually search and make decisions

🎯

High-Intent Treatment Campaigns

Targeting patients researching specific procedures. When someone searches "is radiofrequency ablation right for me," they see ads speaking directly to that treatment, leading to landing pages that address their specific concerns.

🚨

Emergency Response Campaigns

Capturing patients in acute pain searching during off-hours. Mobile-preferred ads with click-to-call extensions and messaging about same-day or next-day availability.

Condition-Specific Campaigns

Organized by pain type—chronic back pain, neuropathic pain, arthritis pain, migraine treatment. Each campaign speaks to that specific condition's symptoms and available treatments.

⚔️

Competitive Conquest Campaigns

Targeting patients searching for your competitors by name, positioning your clinic as a better alternative with shorter wait times or more advanced treatments.
